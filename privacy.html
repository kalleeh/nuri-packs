Privacy Policy for Nuri

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Nuri is a learning app for young children, built by an independent parent developer. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with data.

We do not collect personal data

Nuri does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no registration. We do not ask for names, email addresses, photos, contacts, or location.

No tracking or analytics

Nuri contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, no cookies, and no advertising identifiers (IDFA). We do not build profiles and we do not share anything with third parties, because we never collect anything to share.

Your child's progress stays on the device

Learning progress, stars, streaks, drawings, coloring, and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. This information is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

The one network request Nuri makes

To offer optional extra coloring packs without an app update, Nuri can download a list of available packs and their images from https://kalleeh.github.io/nuri-packs (a static GitHub Pages site). This is a one-way download of public artwork: no personal data, device identifiers, or usage information is ever sent in these requests. Standard web-server logs at that host may record the IP address making a request, as with any website; we do not use those logs to identify or track users.

Children's privacy

Nuri is intended to be used by young children together with a parent or guardian. Because the app collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or any age).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
